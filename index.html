FRIED RICE



Description

Fried rice is a delicious Nigerian delicacy, best served with chicken and a cold drink

  • 3 Cups Of Ricee
  • Raw Chicken
  • 3-4 Cups Of Chicken Stock
  • 300g Boneless Chicken
  • Vegetable Oil
  • 2 Green Bell Pepper Chopped
  • 3 Cups Green Peas
  • 2 Cup Long Beans
  • 2 Cups Sweetcorn
  • 3 Carrots Chopped
  • 1 Cup Cooked/Raw Prawn
  • 2 Cups Diced CookedLiver/Kidney
  • Ginger and Garlic Paste
  • Curry Powder
  • Thyme
  • Garlic Powder
  • Chicken Seasoning
  • Smoked Paprika
  • Salt to Taste

Cooking Steps

  1. Season Your Chicken with: Onions,Thyme,Salt,Curry Powder,Maggi Cubes
  2. Add some water into the chicken stock
  3. Boil the chicken till its soft
  4. Season Your Boneless Chicken With; Thyme,Maggi,Curry Powder,Paprika
  5. Leave to marinate for 30 mins
  6. Add your carrots,sweetcorn,Green Peas,Green Pepper,and season with salt,thymeand garlic powder in a bowl
  7. Leave the Vegetable to marinate for 30 mins
  8. In a pot, add vegetable oil
  9. Add your thoroughly washed rice to the pot
  10. Add your chicken stock to the rice with some water
  11. Add vegetable oil to a pan
  12. Add your diced meat to your pan and fry to fully cooked
  13. Use the oil you used to fry your meat to fry your prawns
  14. Remove your prawns from the pan
  15. Add garlic&ginger paste to your prawn stock
  16. Fry for 2 mins
  17. Add onions to your fried garlic paste along with your vegetables
  18. Add your fried meant and liver to ur vegetables
  19. Pour your rice into the vegetable and meat.Let it simmer for 3 mins
  20. AND YOUR NIGERIAN FRIED RICE IS READY.ENJOY!!!!
